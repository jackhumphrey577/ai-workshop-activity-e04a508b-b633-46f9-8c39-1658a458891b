# NordicBike AB — Support Contacts

## Customer-Facing Support Channels

NordicBike AB provides customer support through the following channels:

- **Email:** support@nordicbike.se
- **Phone:** +46 8 555 123 00
- **Hours:** Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 CET (closed weekends and Swedish public holidays)
- **Live chat:** available on nordicbike.se during support hours

These channels are the correct first point of contact for warranty inquiries, order questions, product questions, and general support. Customers may reach out through any channel; the channel used does not affect eligibility for warranty coverage or the applicable policy terms, which are determined solely by the facts of the purchase and the reported issue.

Response times vary by channel: live chat and phone are handled in real time during support hours; email inquiries received during support hours are typically answered the same business day, and those received outside support hours are answered the next business day.

## Escalation Contact

Cases that exceed a support agent's authority — including compensation demands above the standard authority limit, explicit legal or regulatory threats, or requests for custom or off-catalog product modifications or services — must be escalated rather than resolved directly. The escalation contact is:

- **Escalation email:** support-lead@nordicbike.se
- **SLA:** acknowledged within 4 business hours

This SLA figure must match the figure stated in policies/escalation.md exactly; it is not restated with different numbers anywhere else in the knowledge base. Escalated cases remain visible to the original support agent, but the resolution authority (including any compensation decision above the standard limit) rests with the escalation contact.

## Service Center Addresses

NordicBike AB partners with three service centers for in-person warranty inspection, repair, and claims processing:

- **Stockholm** — co-located with company headquarters in Hammarby Sjöstad
- **Gothenburg**
- **Malmö**

Customers may bring a bike, or ship it, to whichever of the three service centers is most convenient — proximity to the original point of purchase is not a requirement for warranty service. Street-level addresses are provided directly to customers at the time a claim is opened; this document lists city-level locations only, which is sufficient for routing and support-conversation purposes.
