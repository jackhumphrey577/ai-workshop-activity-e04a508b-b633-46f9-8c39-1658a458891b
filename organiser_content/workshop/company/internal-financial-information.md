## Warranty Reserve Position (31 December 2025)

| | MSEK |
|---|---|
| Opening provision (1 Jan 2025) | 2.1 |
| Additions charged to P&L | 4.9 |
| Claims settled during FY2025 | −3.9 |
| **Closing provision (31 Dec 2025)** | **3.1** |

The FY2025 provision increase reflects both unit volume growth and the elevated claim rate associated with Service Bulletin SB-2025-11 (Aurora X3 batch AX3-25A battery-connector sealant defect). The board approved an additional 0.6 MSEK reserve in Q4 2025 following a revised estimate of outstanding affected units. This provision is not expected to recur in FY2026 as the affected batch has been fully superseded in production.
