# NordicBike AB — About

## Overview

NordicBike AB is a Stockholm-based electric bicycle company founded in 2019. The company designs and sells e-bikes engineered specifically for Nordic conditions — cold temperatures, wet roads, long dark winters, and daily commuting distances that stretch well beyond what a typical city bike is built for. NordicBike sells directly to consumers through its online store and supports customers through three partner service centers located in Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö. The company has grown from a small workshop operation to a team of approximately 85 employees spanning product engineering, customer support, logistics, and retail partnerships, while keeping its entire operational footprint inside Sweden.

NordicBike's product range spans city commuting, cargo transport, and winter-specific riding, each addressed by a dedicated model rather than a single do-everything bike. The company also sells spare and replacement batteries and a small catalog of riding accessories, so that a customer's relationship with NordicBike doesn't end at the point of sale — batteries wear out, accessories get added over time, and bikes need warranty support years after purchase. Support quality and warranty clarity are treated internally as part of the product, not an afterthought bolted on after the sale.

## Founding & History

NordicBike AB was founded in 2019 by a small group of engineers and cyclists in Stockholm who were frustrated with e-bikes designed for milder European climates being sold, unmodified, into the Swedish market. The founding team set out to build electric bikes that would perform reliably through a Nordic winter: batteries that tolerate cold better, tires and drivetrains suited to snow and salt-treated roads, and a warranty and service model that assumes the bike will be used outdoors, year-round, in a climate most manufacturers treat as an edge case.

The company's headquarters has been located in Hammarby Sjöstad, Stockholm, since its founding — a neighborhood chosen partly for its own cycling infrastructure, which the founding team used as an informal testing ground for early prototypes. Over the following years NordicBike expanded from a single flagship city e-bike into a small but focused product line, and built out a partner service center network so that customers outside the capital region would not need to ship a bike back to Stockholm for routine warranty work.

## Locations

NordicBike AB's headquarters and primary office are located in Hammarby Sjöstad, Stockholm, Sweden. The company does not operate its own retail storefronts; all direct sales go through the online store at nordicbike.se. Physical service is provided through three partner service centers:

- Stockholm (co-located with company HQ in Hammarby Sjöstad)
- Gothenburg
- Malmö

Each service center is authorized to receive bikes for warranty inspection, repair, and claims processing, regardless of where the customer originally purchased the bike.

## Mission Statement

NordicBike AB exists to make electric mobility the practical everyday choice across the Nordics, engineered for Nordic winters and built to last.
